## 1. Foundational Philosophy: Radical Commercial Clarity

Traditional software development agencies frequently burden clients with hidden overheads, unpredictable time-and-materials invoices, and opaque technical jargon.

Rishvin Labs is built upon an alternative standard: **Radical Commercial Clarity**.
- Every service scope is defined by concrete deliverables, not vague hourly promises.
- Starting price points are openly published to establish realistic budgets from day one.
- Clients retain 100% intellectual property ownership upon full milestone payment.
- Third-party costs are never bundled or marked up for arbitrage.

## 2. The ₹300 Entry-Level Micro-Task Policy

To demystify engineering and provide immediate support to website owners, Rishvin Labs offers starting micro-tasks at ₹300.

### What ₹300 Explicitly Includes:
- Exactly **one (1)** discrete modification to an existing, functioning, and accessible codebase.
- Examples: Correcting a misspelled headline, updating a phone number or email address, fixing a broken outbound hyperlink, swapping an image asset, or fixing a minor CSS alignment flaw on a single element.
- Verified in staging and redeployed to production.

### What ₹300 Explicitly Excludes:
- Multi-page layout redesigns or architectural refactoring.
- Debugging corrupted database tables or recovering lost hosting credentials.
- Writing new custom backend endpoints or integrating new third-party APIs.
- Resolving server crashes or domain DNS propagation failure caused by unpaid hosting bills.

## 4. Third-Party Costs & Direct Pass-Through Rules

Rishvin Labs strictly separates professional engineering services from external infrastructure and software subscription charges.

### Pass-Through Expense Categorization:
1. **Domains & DNS:** Registered directly under the client's account (e.g., Namecheap, Cloudflare Registrar, Route 53). Rishvin Labs provides configuration assistance at no extra markup.
2. **Cloud Hosting & Compute:** Billed directly to the client's cloud provider (e.g., Vercel, AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, Supabase, Railway).
3. **AI & Machine Learning Inference:** API token usage for OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or Groq is charged directly to the client's API keys.
4. **Telephony & Messaging:** WhatsApp Cloud API conversation charges (Meta), Twilio SMS fees, or SendGrid email relays are paid directly to the providers.
5. **Payment Processing:** Payment gateway gateway fees (e.g., 2% Razorpay transaction fee, 2.9% Stripe fee) are deducted by the processor at the merchant level.

## 5. Milestone Billing & Invoicing Standards

To ensure complete fairness and avoid upfront risk for both parties, Rishvin Labs enforces structured milestone billing:

### Project Milestones Schedule:
- **Small Tasks (under ₹5,000):**
  - 100% upon written confirmation or staging approval prior to final production release.
- **Standard Projects (₹5,000 to ₹30,000):**
  - **Milestone 1 (50%):** Mobilization deposit required to reserve sprint schedule and initiate architectural scaffolding.
  - **Milestone 2 (50%):** Payable upon complete staging acceptance, functional verification, and prior to production domain DNS handover.
- **Custom Milestone Projects (₹30,000+):**
  - **Milestone 1 (30%):** Project initiation, architecture design, and database schema approval.
  - **Milestone 2 (30%):** Core functional staging demo demonstrating CRUD operations and critical user journeys.
  - **Milestone 3 (40%):** Final QA smoke testing, production release, documentation handover, and code repository transfer.

## 6. Scope Variation & Change Request Protocol

During any digital engineering initiative, new ideas and requirement adjustments inevitably emerge.

### Protocol for Handling Scope Adjustments:
1. **Minor Tweaks (Complimentary):** Small font-weight adjustments, minor color tint corrections, or small copy edits during active staging review are completed at zero additional charge.
2. **Substantive Additions (Formal Change Request):**
   - Any request for additional pages, new API integrations, unexpected database relationships, or altered user flows outside the signed Statement of Work is documented in a **Change Request (CR)**.
   - The CR details: (a) Technical description, (b) Fixed implementation cost (from our standard add-ons rate card), and (c) Impact on project delivery timeline.
   - Work on the addition begins only after the client approves the CR in writing.

## 7. 14-Day Post-Launch Defect Warranty

Every fixed-scope project delivered by Rishvin Labs includes a complimentary **14-Calendar-Day Defect Warranty**.

### Warranty Terms:
- **Coverage:** If any software defect, broken hyperlink, responsive CSS misalignment, or functional regression is discovered that deviates from the approved project specification, Rishvin Labs will triage and patch the defect promptly at zero additional charge.
- **Response Window:** Defect reports during the warranty window receive priority 24-business-hour response.
- **Exclusions:** Modifications, deletions, or configuration changes introduced by third-party contractors, unauthorized client staff, or major breaking updates to third-party APIs during the warranty window.

## 8. Ongoing Maintenance & Retainer Engagements

Post-launch maintenance retainers provide dedicated monthly developer capacity to guarantee system stability and evolution.

- **Unused Hours Rollover:** Unused developer hours in any given billing cycle roll over for up to 30 calendar days.
- **Cancellation Policy:** Maintenance retainers operate on month-to-month contracts. Clients may cancel or pause retainers with 14 calendar days written notice before the next monthly billing cycle.
